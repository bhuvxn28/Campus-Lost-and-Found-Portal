About Our Portal

The Campus Lost & Found Portal helps students report, search and recover lost belongings within the university. Instead of scattered WhatsApp groups, everything lives in one place and updates in real time.

Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with Firebase Firestore as the cloud database and Vercel for hosting.

How It Works

1

Report

Submit details of your lost or found item.

2

Search

Browse available items by category.

3

Match

Compare item details to find possible matches.

4

Recover

Contact the owner and collect the item safely.
